# BeatMarker Showcase

## Intent

A grounded product-demo film for BeatMarker, an After Effects CEP panel. The film uses the supplied screenshots as the primary evidence. It shows the visible workflow: loading edit audio, waveform preview, BPM override, start offset, detection sensitivity, Analyze beats, Write beat markers, and the resulting markers visible in the AE timeline screenshot.

## Assets

- `assets/beatmarker-panel.png` — focused panel screenshot.
- `assets/after-effects-workspace.png` — After Effects workspace screenshot with panel and timeline markers.

## Customizations

- Dark premium creative-software aesthetic with amber from the real UI plus restrained cyan/violet accents.
- Voiceover uses the local Kokoro engine; the generated take is 24.1 seconds and sits over a 30-second visual cut.
- Captions are authored as concise scene-level text and follow the short narration beats.

## Truth Boundary

Do not claim perfect detection, automatic editing, transitions, effect synchronization, performance metrics, or time savings. Static screenshots must be presented as interface demonstrations, not fabricated live interaction.
